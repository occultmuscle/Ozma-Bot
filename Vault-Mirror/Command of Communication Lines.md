## 0 · PURPOSE — A TRAINABLE DISCIPLINE
This doctrine exists to be *trained*. Its product is an operator who can hold a communication line at the right tempo: warm, current, receptive, and under command. Avoiding the two failures that wreck a channel:
- **Flooding (Overrun)** — over-communicating a line: chasing, geeking, saturating it until tone collapses and the receiver tunes out. Flood-state behavior leaking into ordinary contact.
- **Lagging (Drift)** — under-communicating a line: letting the interval stretch until the line goes cool and the relationship quietly dies of neglect.
Both are the same error in different clothing: **communication driven by mood instead of command.** You flood the lines you *feel like* working and lag the ones you do not. The crowd lives this way. A Commander does not.

The cure is discipline, run through the **CDRA ladder**:
- **Control** — the ability to *start* a pulse, *change* its tone or interval, and *stop* it (Block, silence phase). A line you cannot stop is a line that commands you.
- **Discipline** — holding cadence whether or not the impulse is present. Discipline is not willpower summoned in the moment; it is **structure set in advance**. The disciplined operator does not *remember* to keep a line warm — the schedule remembers for him.
- **Responsibility** — owning every line in the field. A cool line is not the other party's fault; it is your unkept interval. You are cause over the field, not effect of it.
- **Authority** — what the first three earn. Tempo and tone command over a channel is granted only to the operator who has demonstrated Control, Discipline, and Responsibility. Authority is the *output* of rhythm, never the input.

**Plainly:** discipline becomes real only when it is *scheduled*. Reminders and a cadence calendar are not crutches — they are how Control and Discipline are externalized so one operator can hold many lines without flooding the ones he enjoys or lagging the ones he avoids. Build the schedule that decides *when* you communicate before you communicate more. The **Comm Lag** reminder queue and the twice-daily **Comm Muster** (§6) are the instruments of this discipline — wire them first.

> **Train the rhythm before the message.** A disciplined empty channel can be filled; a flooded one cannot be drained.

## 1 · FOUNDATIONAL LAW — COMMUNICATION AS FORCE
Communication is the directed movement of will and awareness between living Sources.
Each act of attention shapes the surrounding field. Where awareness overlaps, a **Line** forms. When both Sources recognize that line, a **Link** is born. Through it, Force flows — speech, emotion, art, or command.
**Source → Field → Line → Target → Link → Feedback → Interval**
Command, influence, and leadership rest upon this rhythmic movement of force.

---

## 2 · POLARITY OF CONTACT — LINK AND BLOCK
| State | Definition | Purpose | Energy Condition |
|-------|------------|---------|------------------|
| **Link** | Mutual recognition permitting exchange | Circulation of awareness; life and creation | Warm, active, feedback present |
| **Block** | Recognized line deliberately sealed | Defense, containment, energy discipline | Awareness retained; current redirected |

To **Link** is to exchange life. To **Block** is to defend it. **Neglect** (no awareness) and **Hostility** (corrupted exchange) both represent loss of command.

## 3 · PULSE TIERS, LAG & INTERVAL
> *"Before there can be rhythm, there must be motion."*
**Pulse Tier** is the measure of how much Force and Awareness move in a single act — from the first spark of intent to the final receipt of feedback. Each tier represents a different degree of cognitive load, emotional charge, and temporal investment. Tier determines not just duration, but purpose.

| Tier | Name | Duration | ≈ Words (spoken) | ≈ Words (read) | Function | Common Mediums |
|------|------|----------|-------------------|-----------------|----------|----------------|
| **T0** | Reflex / Pulse | 0.5–2 s | <5 / Gesture | <10 / Symbol | Presence — signal, awareness spark | Gesture, glance, emoji, like/reaction, read-receipt |
| **T1** | Micro / Cue | 2–25 s | 5–60 | 10–100 | Directive — command, hook | Spoken command, text/SMS, caption, comment, headline |
| **T2** | Mini / Exchange | 25–90 s | 60–200 | 100–350 | Exchange — smallest full back-and-forth; base unit of trust | Conversation turn, DM, voice note, short comment |
| **T3** | Standard / Drill | 1.5–4 min | 200–600 | 350–1,000 | Reasoning — one idea in motion | Conversation, phone call, short post/reel, email, brief |
| **T4** | Extended / Operation | 4–8 min | 600–1,100 | 1,000–1,900 | Operation — a full developed account or task (1 Moment) | Storytelling, tutorial video, voice memo, one-pager, pitch |
| **T5** | Full / Lesson | 8–18 min | 1,100–2,500 | 1,900–4,300 | Address — a multi-point case; argument or briefing | Presentation, long video, podcast segment, article, sales call |
| **T6** | Lecture / Session | 18–60 min | 2,500–9,000 | 4,300–15,000 | Authority — sustained discourse that holds a group | Livestream, class, webinar, keynote, podcast episode, chapter |
| **T7** | Seminar | 1–2 hr | 9,000–18,000 | 15,000–30,000 | Framework — advanced discussion | Seminar, workshop, panel, long-form podcast, white paper |
| **T8** | Field Module | 2–5 hr | 18,000–42,000 | 30,000–70,000 | Applied — immersive, hands-on transmission | Training day, practicum, course module, manual, documentary |
| **T9** | Doctrine | 5–13 hr | 42,000–110,000 | 70,000–185,000 | System creation / transmission | Book, full course, series, doctrine volume, season |

**The ladder bands.** T0–T3 = tactical (fast, responsive, operational). T4–T6 = strategic (structured, meaning-bearing). T7–T9 = **doctrinal** (framework, transmission, system creation).

**Readiness & Position — the two gates on tier selection.** Which tier you may *send* is bounded twice, and the right tier is the **lower** of the two:
- **Readiness (the receiver's gate)** — can the Target comprehend and *duplicate* this tier?
- **Position (your gate)** — are you *permitted* to originate at this tier? Set by the authority gradient.

**Flood / Filibuster — the off-ladder state.** Not a tier but a *condition*: running a higher tier than the moment warrants, or stretching a pulse to hold the channel and deny the other Source a turn.

**Reciprocity — Matching the Return.** Between peers — match the tier. Across an authority gradient — reduce the tier, but hold the floor. Never fall below the Duplication Floor (roughly T1).

**Escalation — The Climb.** Open low. Climb only on grounded tiers. Never skip a rung, never stall on one.

**T0 Accumulation**  
Ten consecutive T0-only contacts on a line (reactions, likes, read-receipts, bare emojis) with no T1 or higher from either side flags the line.  
- First flag: operator must originate at least one T1 (Micro) on the next pulse, or explicitly Block.  
- If the next three contacts remain T0-only after the flag: demote the line one Sphere (inner → mid, mid → outer).  
- Repeated flags without a T1+ close the climb and move the line to the outer sphere or Block until a real exchange reopens it.

This keeps T0 useful as a pulse while preventing a line from looking warm when it is only being acknowledged. It matches the existing Duplication Floor and Lagging (Drift) language.
