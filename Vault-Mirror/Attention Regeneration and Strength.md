# PART I — REGENERATION

## The finding that should change your scheduling

The 2022 micro-break meta-analysis (22 samples, 2,335 participants):

- **Vigor: d = .36** — significant
- **Fatigue reduction: d = .35** — significant
- **Performance: d = .16 — NOT significant**

Short breaks (≤10 min) **reliably restore how you feel** and **do not reliably restore what you can do.** Performance effects reached significance only for *low-demand* tasks, and the meta-regression found the longer the break, the greater the performance boost — recovery from highly depleting work needs more than ten minutes.

**Doctrinal consequence.** Feeling restored is not being restored. A crew member reporting "I'm good" after six minutes is reporting accurately about vigor and inaccurately about capacity. **G4's 5-Moment rest is not generous. It is the minimum the evidence supports.**

## The Regeneration Ladder

### R0 — Micro-Reset · 30 sec–5 min · Cyclic Sighing

The fastest evidence-backed intervention available. A randomized controlled trial (Balban, Huberman & Spiegel; *Cell Reports Medicine*, 2023) compared three 5-minute daily breathwork protocols against 5 minutes of mindfulness meditation over one month.

**Cyclic sighing — the exhale-emphasized protocol — beat mindfulness meditation** on mood improvement and respiratory rate reduction. Effects measurable **after a single 5-minute session**, compounding over 28 days.

**Execution:** double inhale through the nose (full inhale, then a short second sniff to top off), then a long slow exhale through the mouth until empty. Repeat 1–5 minutes.

**Use for:** transition into a container; after a FLASH item; before a hard conversation. Under 1 Moment. Doctrinally free.

### R1 — Micro-Break · ≤10 min (1–2 Moments)

Restores vigor and reduces fatigue reliably. Does **not** reliably restore performance after demanding work. Correct after G1–G3; insufficient after G4.

**Quality rule:** a break on the same input channel is not a break. Screen-work followed by phone-scrolling changes the content but not the load. Change the channel: stand, move, look far, go outside, drink water, speak to someone present.

### R2 — Nap · 10 min or 26 min · Choose deliberately

| Duration      | Onset     | Benefit                                                                                           | Sleep inertia                                           |
| ------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **10 min**    | Immediate | Best *immediate* alertness gain; persists up to ~2.5 hr                                           | None                                                    |
| **20–26 min** | Delayed   | Larger performance gain — NASA's pilot study found **+54% alertness, +34% performance** at 26 min | Mild                                                    |
| **≥30 min**   | Delayed   | —                                                                                                 | **Significant** — higher probability of slow-wave sleep |

**Rule:** must be sharp immediately → take 10. Have a **Grace Shift** to absorb inertia → take 26. Never drift past 30 without intending a full cycle.

### R3 — Movement · acute aerobic

Emergency countermeasure and scheduled asset. **Short-duration moderate-to-high intensity exercise significantly improves inhibitory control in the sleep-deprived** — the correct response to a degraded Watch, ahead of additional caffeine.

Intensity is a dial with known direction: **high → best immediate vigilance. Low → better working memory.** Choose by what the next block requires.

### R4 — The Nightly Close · psychological detachment

Sonnentag's stressor-detachment research establishes psychological detachment as a strong recovery mechanism. Lack of detachment **impairs sleep quality**, driving exhaustion and rising recovery need; sleep deprivation partially mediates the path from poor detachment to depleted mental energy.

Detachment requires closed loops. An open loop keeps bidding and keeps residue circulating. **The Facsimile render at Maghrib and the CUB close at Isha are not merely record-keeping — they are the detachment mechanism.** Seeding tomorrow's Primary hands the open loop to a document so it stops occupying the operator.

**Standing correction:** a Watch ending without a closed CUB degrades the *following* Watch through impaired detachment and sleep. **The cost is not one Watch. It is two.**

### R5 — The Four Recovery Experiences · weekly and above

Sonnentag & Fritz identify four — only the first two match the usual picture of "rest":

1. **Detachment** — switching off
2. **Relaxation** — low activation
3. **Control** — deciding for yourself how time is spent
4. **Mastery** — challenging activity in an *unrelated* domain

**Mastery is the counterintuitive one and the most doctrinally useful.** Hard training in an unrelated domain is *recovery*, not additional load. Mat work, Arabic, a build project, homestead labour — these recover the operator from doctrine work while being demanding in themselves. This dissolves the false binary between working and resting.

**Control** is why externally-imposed rest restores less than chosen rest — an argument for the Grade-declaration mechanic. An operator who *declares* their own Grade recovers better than one assigned it.

# PART II — STRENGTH

## S1 — Mindfulness-Based Attention Training (MBAT)

The best-evidenced deliberate attention training that exists — and **the evidence base was built on military cohorts under predeployment stress**, not undergraduates in quiet rooms.

Amishi Jha's Army-funded program (Univ. of Miami; USAMRDC) established:

- **Without intervention, attention and working memory degrade** across a high-demand interval, and lapses increase. Degradation is the *default* under operational stress — holding steady is already a win.
- **Marines practicing ≥12 minutes daily did not merely preserve working memory under predeployment stress — they improved.** Clear dose-response.
- **Minimum effective dose: 12–15 minutes, 3–4 days per week.** The MBAT course is 8 hours over 4 weeks.
- Later work compressed it to **8 hours over 8 weeks** for operational personnel, finding the variant emphasizing *practice* outperformed the variant emphasizing *didactic content about* stress and resilience.
- **Instructors familiar with the population outperformed mindfulness experts who were not.** Soldiers trained by military-savvy instructors showed fewer lapses and fewer difficulties managing emotions.

**Three doctrinal readings, in order of importance:**

1. **Practice beats explanation.** Reading this manual is not the intervention; the twelve minutes are.
2. **Native delivery beats expert delivery.** Attention training transmitted inside the crew's own language outperforms generic instruction. Teaching this in Hegemony terms is an empirical performance finding, not a stylistic preference.
3. **Twelve minutes is two Moments.** The minimum effective dose of the best-supported attention training in existence costs **2 Moments, 3–4 times per Cycle** — a 1.25% allocation against a 160-Moment waking day, for the only known intervention that raises the baseline.

### The rep

**The repetition is not staying focused. The repetition is noticing you left and returning.**

Place attention on a chosen anchor — breath is standard. Attention will leave. It always leaves; that is the machine working normally. The moment you *notice* and bring it back is one rep. A session where the mind wandered forty times and was returned forty times is a set of forty. A session where it never wandered is not better — it is a shorter set.

Jha's military-facing app is named *Pushups for the Mind*. The framing is exact: **the return is the curl.** Wandering is the load, not the failure.

**This is [[Hegemony Standard Time]]'s Resistance, from the other side.** Re-Seating is the rep; Resistance is its felt cost. Every Hour-Mark is a repetition. **The Watch Log is a training log.**

### Progression

| Stage | Dose | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | 2 Moments (12 min), 3–4 × per Cycle | Focused attention — breath anchor, notice-and-return |
| Build | 2 Moments daily | Same; consistency before duration |
| Extend | 2–3 Moments daily | Add open monitoring — anchor on the whole field |
| Applied | Embedded | Bring notice-and-return into live work: each Hour-Mark is a rep |

**Do not extend duration before establishing frequency.** One 30-minute session weekly does not substitute for four 12-minute sessions.

*Hegemony note:* five daily prayers already impose five structured attentional returns per day, at a cadence more consistent than most formal protocols achieve. This is attention training that happens to be worship — never worship repurposed as attention training. The order matters, the doctrine already has it right, and the training benefit is a consequence, not a justification.

## S2 — Chunk Drilling

Capacity is hard-limited near four chunks and cannot be enlarged; **density can.** Every sequence rehearsed to automaticity converts a four-slot process into a one-slot process and permanently returns capacity to the field.

**Protocol:** identify one sequence executed weekly at multi-slot cost. Drill it cold — no notes, no prompts — until it runs as one unit. One sequence per Cycle. Gains do not decay the way trained-task gains from cognitive games do, because the chunk is used in live operations rather than only in the training task.

## S3 — Progressive Overload on Interval Length

[[Attention]]'s Interval Scale is a training ladder, not just a scheduling tool. An operator who cannot hold G3 does not need to be told to try harder; they need to be run at G1 and progressed.

**Progression rule:** hold a Grade for a full Cycle with fewer than two down-grades before advancing. G4 is a *capacity* and is use-it-or-lose-it — an operator who hasn't run G4 in an Epoch should expect to re-earn it.

## S4 — Stimulus Discipline (the honest version)

**"Dopamine detox" is not a real mechanism and should not enter Hegemony doctrine under that name.** You cannot detox from a neurotransmitter essential to normal function. The more extreme protocols — enforced isolation, restrictive dieting — carry real physical and mental-health downside.

**What is defensible:** the issue is *overstimulation and habit*, not a chemical needing a reset. Removing high-stimulation inputs does not rewire the reward system; it stops reinforcing a behaviour, and durable gains come from **long-term habit change**, without which people reliably revert. Many who abstain do report renewed capacity for reading, walking, undistracted conversation — that report is real; the neurochemical story attached to it is not.

**Correct framing:** environment design and habit formation, governed by the Attention-Tax Policy, operating by **raising Friction on the Adversary's approach.** Same practice, honest mechanism, no dependence on a claim that will not survive scrutiny.

## S5 — Caffeine: a tool, not a ritual

Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors; its vigilance effects are real. Two honest notes:

- The popular "delay your first coffee 60–90 minutes" rule is **not settled**. A Walter Reed study found personnel taking 200 mg immediately on waking had **18% faster reaction times and 29% higher vigilance scores** than those who delayed. No definitive optimal post-wake time is established; it varies with chronotype and sensitivity.
- Benefit is most pronounced during **circadian troughs** — which maps to the Asr window, already doctrinally identified as the deepest trough and correctly reserved for assessment.

**Standing position:** a legitimate vigilance tool with a known cost — it borrows against sleep, and sleep is capital maintenance on Resource #2. Deploy against a trough. Do not deploy against a sleep deficit; that is R3's job. Do not adopt a timing ritual whose confidence exceeds its evidence.

## Command Summary

- Regeneration restores today. Strength raises the baseline. Budget both, separately.
- **Feeling restored is not being restored.** Short breaks reliably return vigor, not capacity.
- Fastest lever: **cyclic sighing** — under a Moment, measurable from a single session.
- Sharpest lever: **12 minutes of MBAT**, 3–4× per Cycle. Two Moments for the only intervention shown to *raise* attention under operational stress rather than merely defend it.
- **The rep is the return, not the staying.** Wandering is the load. Re-Seating is the rep; Resistance is its felt cost.
- Match the restoration to the failed mode. A nap does not fix an executive failure; a break does not fix an orienting one.
- **The CUB close is a regeneration protocol.** Skipping it costs two Watches, not one.
- Practice beats explanation. Native delivery beats expert delivery. Teach this in Hegemony language — that is a finding, not a preference.
