**The Attention Interval Scale — Time × Intensity**

**Authority:** Hegemon, Starshott Hegemony
**Effective:** Stardate 120260803
**Related:** [[The Attention Codex]] · [[Hegemony Standard Time]] · [[Attention Regeneration and Strength]] · [[Watch-Keeping]]

**Origin:** Field conversation, Praetor × daughter. She said: *"Just schedule me for the hour and let me take rest depending on how hard the focus is."* She was right about the container. The neuroscience sets the ratios.

---

## The Two Axes

[[Hegemony Standard Time]] fixed the atom: **1 Moment = 6 minutes**. What was never built is the scale above it. Attention expenditure varies along two independent axes:

- **Time** — how long the continuous work interval runs.
- **Intensity** — how deep the attention goes.

Rest is not a fixed ration. **Rest is a function of both axes.**

## The Container Rule

> **Scheduling issues containers. Intensity sets the split.**

The commander schedules whole containers. The operator declares an Intensity Grade at container-open. The Grade — not negotiation, not mood — sets the work/rest interval inside it.

## The Interval Scale

| Grade  | Name      | Work / Rest | Moments | Duty cycle | Backing                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------ | --------- | ----------- | ------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **G0** | Atom      | 1 Moment    | 1 + 0   | —          | The ledger grain. Not an interval — the unit intervals are built from.                                                                                                                                 |
| **G1** | Surface   | 20 / 10     | 3 + 2   | 2:1        | Classic Pomodoro band. Attention rises ~20–25 min, then declines. For high-resistance tasks, new skills, young crew, low Field.                                                                        |
| **G2** | Focused   | 24 / 6      | 4 + 1   | 4:1        | The existing Pomodoro Engine (30-min cycle). Routine execution.                                                                                                                                        |
| **G3** | Sustained | 48 / 12     | 8 + 2   | 4:1        | **The clean hour.** More rest per hour than any average job grants. Default scheduled container.                                                                                                       |
| **G4** | Deep Flow | ~150 / 30   | 25 + 5  | 5:1        | **Flow entry = 3–4 Moments (18–24 min)** uninterrupted; interruption resets the ramp. Sustains for hours if challenge–skill balance holds. 2.5 hr is the planning block; the ceiling is physiological. |
| **G5** | Watch     | 8 hr        | 80      | composite  | Not an interval — a composite of G1–G4. **Exactly one Watch** under Day ÷ 3.                                                                                                                           |

**Reading the scale:** as you climb, the interval lengthens and rest converts from *frequent-and-small* to *rare-and-large*. Total rest fraction stays near 17–33% — what changes is continuity. Depth requires continuity; continuity is what rest is traded for.

**Rules:**
- A Grade may be declared **down** mid-container, never **up** — the G4 entry ramp cannot be paid with a container already half-spent.
- **G4 must never be Pomodoro-chopped.** A rest bell inside flow destroys 3–4 Moments of re-entry. Opposite failure mode from G1–G3, where *skipping* rest is the error.
- Driving and monitoring sit **outside** the scale — vigilance degrades with time-on-task regardless of rest ratio, and is governed by rotation, not intervals. Logged as continuous stream (Drive Mode).

> **Feeling restored is not being restored.** Short breaks reliably return vigor; they do not reliably return capacity. G4's 5-Moment rest is not generous — it is the minimum the evidence supports. See [[Attention Regeneration and Strength]].

## The Grace Shift

Borrowed from three-shift continuous operations: shifts run downtime-free because each **overlaps** its relief, and the overlap absorbs the transition cost. HST adopts the same mechanism at personal scale.

A **Grace Shift** is a scheduled overlap block (2–5 Moments) at container boundaries. Not rest, not work — **transition capital.** It pays for:

1. **Entry ramps** — a G4 block preceded by a Grace Shift has its 3–4 Moment ramp *pre-paid*. This is what makes depth reachable inside an otherwise ordinary hour.
2. **Overruns** — a flow state wanting five more Moments can have them without cascading the Watch.
3. **Handoffs** — the overlap when the Watch transfers ([[Watch-Keeping]], Designation of Relief). Shift-handoff research: 15–30 min is standard, and most serious transition errors occur at *unbuffered* handoffs. **HST standard: 3 Moments** — outgoing summary / incoming re-Perceive / joint log-confirm.

**Day-scale — ratified doctrine.** The Day is a 3-Watch rotation: **Work Watch · Life Watch · Sleep Watch**, 80 Moments each. The Grace Shift is the overlap at each boundary; the Salat anchors function as built-in grace shifts inside the Watches. The pattern is fractal: overlap at every boundary, no downtime, no collision. See [[Hegemony Standard Time]] for the full day-split table and coverage math.

## Worked Examples

- **Crew member, schooling:** scheduled a 2-container morning. Declares G1. Runs 20/10 × 4. Same clock time as an adult's G3 hour ×2 — different split, same container. Nothing renegotiated.
- **Praetor, Work Watch open:** declares G4. Grace Shift (3 Moments) → 25-Moment flow block → 5-Moment rest → remainder at G2. One deliverable, no bell interruptions.
- **Ordinary hour, Field low:** declared G3, failing by Moment 4 → drop to G1. Logged as `G3→G1` — a data point on the operator's real capacity curve, not a failure.

## Command Summary

- One fixed atom. Two axes. One rule: containers scheduled, splits set by Grade.
- Rest converts from frequent-small to rare-large as depth increases.
- The Grace Shift is the overlap that makes the ladder run without downtime — at hour boundaries, at Watch handoffs, at day scale.
- She gets her hour. The Grade gets its ratio. The ledger gets both.
