# Paperboy detector research log

Human sound is the standard. Beating a detector is a check, not the goal. When a trick clears a score and makes the prose worse, record it as a failed trick and retire it.

Shared-computer copy: `/workspace/paperboy/detector-research.md`.

## 2026-10-02 — ZeroGPT — Week 7 scholarly sources

- **Detector:** ZeroGPT
- **Score before / after:** 17.5% → 0%
- **What changed:** Five long yellow lines were shortened. Existing short punches were left in place ("Some articles say References." "The trail matters." "Blogs often skip the list." "Peer review is the whole reason." "A thin paper can get bounced." "Journal articles put it up front." "Works Cited is the other trait." "He wrote a Carnivore Diet book." "The label was a sales pitch." plus the one-word "Chiropractor."). Diction went with them ("tells you the fit," "Belief is what is left, with nothing to open").
- **Still sounds human?** No. Assignment structure was fine. The ear failed. Short punches stacked into a cadence.
- **Keep or retire:** Retire. The 3–6 word punch (and one-word beat) is a failed trick. Do not use it to buy a score.


## 2026-10-02 — ZeroGPT — Week 7 scholarly sources, ear-rule rewrite

- **Detector:** ZeroGPT
- **Score before / after:** 88.2% (long parallel trait sentences) → 81.3% → 21.9% → 20.6% → 24.5% (paraphrasing the same funder and Works Cited lines made it worse) → 0%
- **What changed:** Stopped defining the traits in "X is the trait, so the reader can" sentences. Kept peer review, named author/school/lab, and citations. Put the citation part in past-tense concrete steps (scrolled to the PDF, heading said References, opened one study, the blog had no list). Two short sentences only, not in a row: "I went with the journal." and "I opened one." No one-word beat. No cluster of three sentences under eight words.
- **Still sounds human?** Yes, on the pre-send check. Short lines are isolated facts, not a stacked cadence.
- **Keep or retire:** Keep the concrete past-tense shape for citation talk. Retire polishing the same yellow definition line over and over; the score went from 20.6% back up to 24.5% when those lines were only reworded.
