# House Munnies — Dateline Payment Law (operational rule card)

**Cite:** HCO PL 28 Jan 65 (credit/solvency / dateline) — HCO Saint Hill.  
**Installed:** 2026-09-30 by Praetor order via XO → Keeper.  
**L1:** Apply on every pay ticket, LOGSTAT, and Need-line advice. Do **not** store or reprint the full PL.

## Rules

1. **Credit** is confidence and reliability, not cash on hand. Handling wrecks credit more than low income.
2. **Make all you can. Spend less than that.** Speed the income line; slow the disbursement line. Unauthorized buys are personal, not House.
3. **Solvency** = spend less than you make; make it before you spend it; build a cushion and do not raid it; keep credit as second cushion; do not borrow to pay bills; finance must be able to say No.
4. **PAY BY DATELINE ONLY.** Pay every bill up to a chosen date and none closer to present time. Never sprinkle a little on each bill — that advertises a lean period. Cut the dateline back until cash covers **ALL** bills behind it. Then advance the dateline toward PT as cash allows.
5. **Files** must answer: “Are you sure no bill older than this dateline remains?” If one older bill was missed, the unit failed.
6. **EXCEPTIONS:** tax, water, sometimes rent/phone that threaten cutoff — still try dateline first; if you must pay the threat, retard other bills, and thereafter deal with that vendor only on threat terms (current/cash). Private firm that threatens suit or demands extra despite dateline: warn, then stop trading there.
7. Do **not** eager-beaver random bills “to pay our bills.” **Age of bill governs**, not whoever shouts.
8. Know cash and bills within **days**, not weeks.
